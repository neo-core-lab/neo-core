## 4.0.13 (November 2026)

The working NEO, as now running in Firefox, Edge and Chrome.

### How NEO veils
- The main post always stays visible.
- Every reply is veiled, along with all the back and forth beneath it. NEO veils everything equally and uses no harm filter, because NEO does not decide what is harmful.
- Profile photos and handles stay visible.
- Pictures, memes, GIFs and videos inside replies are veiled too.
- Veils use phrases from the local packs only.
- A small ◐ mark (darkness and light) sits in the bottom right corner of every veil. It gently reminds the reader that what they are reading is a NEO veil.

### Platforms
- Instagram

### Doctrine
- No AI of any kind.
- No servers. Everything runs on the device.
- Zero logs, zero storage of user text, zero surveillance.

### Files

- The code files at the root (`content.js`, `content.css`, `neo-mark.css`, `background.js`, `popup.html`, `popup.js`) are updated to 4.0.13. The root `manifest.json` keeps its license header; the working manifest is in `neo-4.0.13/`.
- The pack files at the root of the repository are updated with the same phrases, now saved in clean UTF-8. `dada.json`, `renaissance.json` and `neuroscience_consciousness.json` were damaged and could not be read; they are repaired.
