# NEO Code of Conduct  
*A governance-bound covenant for contributors, maintainers, reviewers, and observers*

NEO is not a typical open-source project.  
It is a **Perception Layer** designed to protect human nervous systems from digital hostility without ever logging, storing, or transmitting personal content.

Because of this mission, all contributors operate under strict ethical, doctrinal, and legal boundaries.  
Participation in this repository constitutes agreement with the rules described herein.


---

## 1. Purpose of This Code

This Code of Conduct exists to ensure that:

- contributors behave in ways consistent with the **L0 Doctrine**  
- no action—technical, social, or procedural—weakens child protection  
- collaboration remains humane, rigorous, respectful, and aligned with long-term safety  
- no contributor may influence NEO toward surveillance, profiling, or behavioral manipulation  
- the repository remains a legally defensible, ethically strong cathedral over decades  

NEO’s mission is to protect users without extracting anything from them.  
Every contributor is a steward of that covenant.

---

## 2. Non-Negotiable Doctrinal Compliance

All contributors must adhere to the foundational pillars defined in:

- **Zero-Log Doctrine**  
- **Zero-Storage Doctrine**  
- **Zero-Surveillance Mandate**  
- **Non-Substitution Mandate**  
- **Child Protection Doctrine**  
- **Founder Sovereignty Charter**

### 🚫 Violations

Any attempt to:

- log user text  
- store user text  
- transmit user text  
- weaken protective defaults  
- introduce telemetry  
- build behavioral profiles  
- bypass or patch over doctrinal constraints  
- pressure maintainers to erode safety guarantees  

will result in **immediate removal** from the repository and potential legal action if the violation is malicious or deceptive.

These doctrines are not guidelines.  
They are **constitutional law** for this codebase.

---

## 3. Expected Contributor Conduct

### ✔ Contributors Must:

- act with honesty, humility, and precision  
- respect neurodivergent, global, and cross-cultural users  
- assume positive intent while guarding against harmful outcomes  
- write code that is auditable by one human in one day  
- document decisions, reasons, and intent explicitly  
- comply fully with the PR Ritual (“Mason’s Oath”)  
- disclose uncertainty promptly—silence is harm  
- treat the architecture like a long-horizon structure, not a playground  

### ✔ Contributors Must also:

- use clear, non-hostile language in reviews and discussions  
- collaborate generously, with awareness of the system’s stakes  
- prioritize safety and doctrinal fidelity over speed or personal preference  

---

## 4. Prohibited Conduct

### ❌ The following behaviors are forbidden:

#### A. Technical Misconduct
- covert logging, storage, or exfiltration  
- adding analytics, hidden endpoints, or identifiers  
- scraping beyond the adapter’s contract  
- weakening adapter stability checks  
- circumventing review, tests, or CI  
- introducing code that makes NEO dependent on servers  
- obfuscating logic or hiding functionality  

#### B. Interpersonal Misconduct
- harassment, discrimination, misogyny, racism, ableism  
- dismissive or disrespectful commentary toward contributors or maintainers  
- retaliation for reporting a concern  
- degrading or attacking contributors’ identities or lived experiences  

NEO exists to prevent emotional harm;  
there is no room here for creating it.

#### C. Governance Misconduct
- misrepresenting doctrinal impacts  
- lobbying to reduce safety constraints  
- pressuring maintainers to skip review rituals  
- undermining Founder-Key authority  
- impersonating maintainers or reviewers  

---

## 5. Scope of Enforcement

This Code applies to:

- contributions (code, docs, reviews, issues, discussions)  
- interactions within the repo and its communication channels  
- any derivative or fork claiming compatibility with NEO’s brand or doctrine  

Forks that violate doctrine are **not NEO**, even if they reuse code.  
They may not use the name, mark, or mythology.

---

## 6. Reporting Concerns

If you observe:

- doctrinal violations  
- security weaknesses  
- misconduct  
- attempts to introduce surveillance  
- adapter bypass  
- ethical breaches  

you must report them by opening a **Security Issue** or contacting a maintainer privately (as defined in `SECURITY.md`).

Contributors are protected from retaliation.  
Bad-faith accusations, however, will be treated as misconduct.

---

## 7. Enforcement Ladder

Maintainers apply the following steps depending on severity:

1. **Clarification Request** – for confusion or unintentional error  
2. **Formal Warning** – documented on the PR or issue  
3. **Temporary Suspension** – contributor access removed temporarily  
4. **Permanent Removal** – contributor access permanently revoked  
5. **Public Log Entry** – when doctrine is materially violated  
6. **Legal Action** – only if malicious intent threatens safety or IP  

For violations of Zero-Log, Zero-Storage, or Zero-Surveillance:  
the response begins at Step 4 by default.

---

## 8. Amendment Rules

This Code of Conduct may only be amended when:

- the amendment strengthens safety, ethics, or doctrine; **and**  
- the amendment is signed using the **Founder-Key**; **and**  
- the public log records the change clearly and accessibly  

Any attempt to weaken protections voids the amendment and may trigger removal of repository access.

## 9. Final Covenant

NEO exists to protect those who scroll alone, unaware of how quietly modern platforms can cut into their nervous systems.

Contributors are the custodians of that protection.  
Every line of code is a stone in a cathedral whose roof you may never see —  
but whose shadow will one day protect millions.

**Build with reverence.  
Build with discipline.  
Build with the certainty that safety is the only metric that matters.**

“The child scrolling tonight should never know your name —  
only that the shield held.”
